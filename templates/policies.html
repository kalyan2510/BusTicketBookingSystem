01 · Booking

Booking & seat holds

When you select seats, BUSGO places a temporary hold on them for {{ reservation_minutes }} minutes while you complete passenger details and payment. This prevents two travellers paying for the same seat.

  • You can book up to {{ max_seats }} seats in a single reservation.
  • Seats are only confirmed after successful payment and server-side verification.
  • If payment is not completed in time, the hold expires automatically and the seats return to sale.
  • Passenger names must match a valid government photo ID carried during travel.
  • A confirmed e-ticket with a unique ticket number and QR code is issued instantly.
02 · Payments

Payments & security

BUSGO supports UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, QR, debit card, credit card and net banking. In this deployment the default gateway is a clearly-labelled simulated gateway for demonstration; Razorpay test mode can be enabled by an administrator.

  • We never store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or banking password.
  • All payment results are verified on the server before a booking is confirmed.
  • Fares include a per-seat convenience fee and applicable GST, both shown before you pay.
  • Payment records are retained for accounting, dispute resolution and refund processing.
03 · Cancellation

Cancellation policy

Cancellations are calculated from the scheduled departure time and applied automatically. The refund percentage is based on the total amount paid.

When you cancelRefundRetained as cancellation fee
24 hours or more before departure90%10%
Between 6 and 24 hours before departure50%50%
Less than 6 hours before departureNot permitted—
  • You can cancel from My bookings while the booking is upcoming and eligible.
  • Completed, expired or already-cancelled bookings cannot be cancelled again.
  • If an operator cancels a trip, you receive a full refund regardless of the timeline above.
04 · Refunds

Refund policy

Approved refunds are initiated automatically to your original payment method as soon as the cancellation is processed.

  • UPI and wallet refunds usually arrive within 3–5 working days.
  • Card and net-banking refunds can take 5–7 working days depending on your bank.
  • Convenience fees are refunded only when BUSGO or the operator cancels the trip.
  • You will see the refund amount and status against the booking in your dashboard.
05 · Travel

Travel & boarding rules

  • Carry a valid photo ID matching the lead passenger's name. Operators may deny boarding without it.
  • Report at the boarding point at least 15 minutes before departure. Missed buses are non-refundable.
  • Alcohol, smoking, and prohibited substances are not allowed on any BUSGO service.
  • Luggage allowances are set by the operator; valuables should be carried with you.
  • Children above 5 years require their own seat and ticket.
06 · Terms

Terms of service

By using BUSGO you agree to provide accurate information, use the platform lawfully, and not attempt to disrupt seat inventory, payments or other travellers' bookings.

  • BUSGO acts as a booking platform connecting travellers with independent bus operators.
  • Schedules, amenities and vehicle type are provided by operators and may change due to operational needs.
  • Fraudulent bookings, chargebacks or automated abuse may result in account suspension.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of India, with jurisdiction in Bengaluru, Karnataka.
07 · Privacy

Privacy policy

We collect only what is needed to issue tickets, process payments and support your journey: your name, email, phone, passenger details and booking history.

  • We do not sell your personal data. Ever.
  • Data is shared with the operating bus company only to the extent required for your trip.
  • Passwords are stored using Django's salted hashing; payment credentials are never stored.
  • You may request access, correction or deletion of your account data by contacting support.
  • Cookies are used for login sessions and essential site functionality only.
